How it works

A simple marketplace workflow.

Buildly is designed around two people: the project owner who needs construction work and the contractor who can deliver it.

1

Client posts a project

Add the project type, description, location, services needed, photos and an optional budget.

2

Contractors discover it

Relevant contractors can browse project opportunities based on services and service areas.

3

Contractors respond

A contractor can express interest and provide an initial message, estimated cost and duration.

For clients

More visibility before you choose.

Compare contractor profiles, services, experience, portfolio and initial responses before deciding who to contact.

Buildly's MVP is a marketplace and discovery layer. Contracts, payments, permits and final project terms remain between the parties unless future Buildly services say otherwise.
For contractors

Turn capacity into opportunities.

Create a profile showing your skills, service areas, experience, portfolio and current availability, then discover projects that fit.

MVP roadmap

Start simple, then add intelligence.

Future versions can introduce verified contractor badges, messaging, ratings, payments, supplier services and AI-assisted project matching.
